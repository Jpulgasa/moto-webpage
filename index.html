Some random information

Motocross bikes are designed to be lightweight and nimble, often weighing between 45 to 113 kg (100 to 250 pounds) to help riders handle rough terrains and obstacles efficiently.
Motocross evolved from motorcycle trials competitions in the UK around 1909 and grew popular with organized races starting in the 1920s, spreading to the U.S. in the 1950s.
Engine sizes in motocross bikes vary widely, from 50 cc for beginners and younger riders up to 450 cc for experienced riders, allowing different skill levels to participate in the sport comfortably.
Freestyle Motocross (FMX) is a variation of the sport focused on acrobatic stunts and tricks rather than racing, with riders judged on style, difficulty, and crowd reaction—a popular event in the X Games since 1999.

"Motocross is not just about speed; it's about the courage to keep pushing, the passion to keep learning, and the heart to rise after every fall.""

— Ricky Carmichael, Motocross Legend.
